# Solar Power Installed at the Matha

The Matha has completed the installation of a rooftop solar array, a step that
brings the daily work of the kitchen and the go-shala onto clean energy.

The system now carries a substantial share of the load that the **annadana** hall
draws each day, when meals are prepared for pilgrims without interruption from
morning until night.

## What the installation covers

- The annadana kitchen and its cold storage
- Lighting across the go-shala
- Water pumping for the cattle sheds

Work was carried out over three weeks, with the panels sited so that they remain
out of view from the temple courtyard and do not disturb the appearance of the
older buildings.

## Why it matters

Reducing the running cost of the kitchen means more of what devotees offer goes
directly to feeding pilgrims. It also reduces the disruption caused by power
cuts during the monsoon, when the kitchen previously depended on a generator.

The Matha is grateful to the devotees whose contributions made the work possible.
